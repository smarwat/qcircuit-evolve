Iteration 0's history bit can be cleared right after iteration 0, so the next iteration reuses its qubit instead of a fresh one. At iteration 0 we have V=p, CU=0 and CV=1, so h1 = a mod 2:
- **a odd:** no swap, CU becomes 1, and the doubled CV is 2.
- **a even:** the registers swap, CU becomes 1, and CV becomes 0.

Either way, bit 1 of the doubled CV equals h1. One `cx(CV[1], h1)` returns the qubit to 0, and the reverse pass undoes that CNOT automatically. Iterations 0 and 1 then share fresh history qubit Hf[0], which saves one ancilla at no Toffoli cost. Expected at n=32: T=34,820 and Q=227, so T*Q is about 7,904,140.
