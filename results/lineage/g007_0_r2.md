The parent swaps the coefficient registers twice per iteration: once in and once back out. This candidate keeps the coefficients in the same persistent swapped orientation as U and V, so one coefficient cswap per iteration, controlled by the delta bit d = s_cur ⊕ s_prev, does the job. Kaliski's step preserves "one coefficient odd, one even", and the just-doubled one (physical CV) is always even. So right after the d-controlled cswap, CV[0] equals d, and three CNOTs clear the spare bit with no stored history. This removes about 1,580 Toffolis from the forward pass, about 3,170 including the uncompute. I expect T ≈ 42,300 and Q = 259 (T*Q ≈ 10.96M) at n=32. I have not run it yet.
